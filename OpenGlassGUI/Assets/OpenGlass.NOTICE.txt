Registry artwork
----------------
The three rectangles and sparkle are adapted from the Microsoft Windows 11
Registry Editor icon. Source artwork: Microsoft Corporation.
No separate redistribution license accompanied the supplied Windows icon.
Its attribution is recorded separately from AeroShotCRE's GPLv3 license
and the MIT license for the previous Fluent-based OpenGlass icon.

OpenGlass modifications (2026-10-24)
-----------------------------------
Recreated the glass window as editable SVG, adjusted its colors and blur,
squared corners, changed the close button to solid red, and moved the
window upward. Added three outlined rectangular registry tiles in an L with
a sparkle, horizontally center-aligned the upper-left tile with the
caption-button divider, and produced PNG and ICO.

Retain this notice and AeroShotCRE.LICENSE.txt with the current icon assets
and their derivatives. The editable source is OpenGlass.svg.
